# rx1-05: Power-up order of 3V3A and 3V0 at the 1-RX ADC inputs (simulation)

## Goal
Show whether the 1-RX ADC (AD9237) analog inputs can go above AVDD + 0.3 V, or pass current
into its input clamps, while the rails come up. U45 (the IF amplifier that drives the ADC) runs
from 3V3A (U24, LD39200) and the ADC from 3V0 (U39, LP5907-3.0); both regulators start from
3V8 with EN tied to 3V8. It holds when the report gives the peak input voltage against AVDD and
the clamp current for each start-up order, against the AD9237 absolute maximum, and a fix when
it fails. (Added 2026-11-24 at the user's request, from the rx1-02 review.)

## Steps
1. Facts, each with its manifest id and page: AD9237 VIN+/VIN- absolute maximum
   AGND - 0.3 to AVDD + 0.3 V (adi-ad9237-datasheet p.7) and that the sheet states no input
   clamp current; the LTC2292's note that its inputs clamp and take more than 100 mA without
   latch-up (adi-ltc2293-2292-2291-datasheet p.2 and p.5, note 3), which is why the 2-RX board
   is the reference; the LP5907 turn-on time 80 typ / 150 max us (ti-lp5907-datasheet p.6); the
   LD39200 start-up from its enable plots (st-ld39200-datasheet p.13, Figures 21-24) and U24's
   bypass capacitor from the netlist. The U45 + inputs sit on the 1.5 V divider R990/R991,
   which is fed from 3V0 (rx1-02), so with 3V0 off the bias is 0 V.
2. `[heavy]` `analysis/15_rx1_powerup/powerup_sim.py`: LTspice transient through `./run --sim`.
   3V8 ramps as the U18 buck starts (its soft start, ti-lmr51420-datasheet); U39 from the TI
   LP5907-3.0 model on file (ti-lp5907-3p0-pspice); U24 as a ramp fitted to the LD39200 plot (ST
   publishes no model, parts/models_needed.csv); U45 the LT6231 subcircuit; the network R97/R99
   49.9 ohm and C163-C165; the AD9237 input as 7 pF per pin with a clamp diode to AVDD and to
   AGND, and AVDD's own load. Cases: both regulators at their typical times; 3V3A first by the
   largest spread the datasheets allow; 3V0 first; each with the mixer IF at zero and at full
   scale. Heavy because: two regulators with no common model set, and the clamp current is
   sensitive to the diode model, which is an assumption to be stated and swept.
3. Pass when, in every case, VIN+/VIN- stay within AGND - 0.3 V and AVDD + 0.3 V; otherwise
   report the peak clamp current and its duration, and say that the AD9237 sheet gives no
   current it tolerates. If a case fails, propose fixes with their cost, for the user to choose:
   U24 EN from 3V0 instead of 3V8 (one net, so 3V3A starts after 3V0), a larger series
   resistance at the ADC inputs (with the settling and IF response of rx1-02 rerun), or a
   Schottky clamp to 3V0.
4. `tests/test_rx1_powerup.py`: the pass check on fixture waveforms, and the ramp fit against the
   plot points read from the LD39200 figure.
5. Report `writeup.md` and `deck.yaml` (no more than 8 slides): the rail waveforms, the ADC input
   voltages against AVDD + 0.3 V per case, the clamp current, the result and any fix. Add the
   result to the 1-RX U8 row of `docs/board-changes.md`. A chosen fix becomes a row of
   `parts/rx1_changes.csv` for rx1-03.

## Acceptance
- Every start-up case of step 2 is shown, with the peak ADC input voltage against AVDD + 0.3 V.
- The diode model and the LD39200 ramp are stated as assumptions, with their sweep.
- A failing case names its fixes and their cost; no fix is applied here.
