# rx1-05: 1-RX power-up and power-down order for the ADC and the FPGA: guaranteed by design, simulated
Status: approved
Goal: parts
Node: any
Deliverable: slides
Accept: user
Verify: ./run uv run pytest tests/test_rx1_powerup.py -q && ./run tools/build_report.py analysis/15_rx1_powerup --check
Hardware: none

## Goal
Show that no AD9237 pin with an AVDD + 0.3 V limit (VIN+, VIN-, CLK, PDWN) goes above that limit,
or passes current into its clamps, while the 1-RX rails come up and go down, with the rail order
set by design: U24 EN (3V3A) and U23 EN (1V8, whose PG enables U25 for 3V3D) on 3V0 from U39
(`rx1-seq` A, the user 2026-11-25). Also show the CLK pin in steady state, where the U6 clock on
3V3D swings to 3.3 V against AVDD 3.0 V. It holds when the report gives, for each case, the peak
voltage at each pin against AVDD + 0.3 V and the clamp current, and a fix with its cost for any
case that fails. It also holds when every ordering the ADC and the FPGA need is listed and marked
either guaranteed by the circuit (an EN or PG chain) or set only by timing, and every timing-only
ordering has a design fix with its cost for the user to choose. (Added 2026-11-24 at the user's
request from the rx1-02 review; rescoped 2026-11-25 by the user to the sequenced design, the CLK
pin and PDWN; widened 2026-11-25 by the user to the whole FPGA power order: orders are to be
guaranteed by design where possible, not left to timing.)

## Steps
1. Facts, each with its manifest id and page: AD9237 VIN+/VIN-, CLK and PDWN absolute maximum
   AGND - 0.3 to AVDD + 0.3 V (adi-ad9237-datasheet p.7), the input circuits (p.10, Figures 4
   and 7: the CLK/PDWN clamp diodes sit at the pin, ahead of 375 ohm) and that the sheet states no
   clamp current; the LTC2292 note that its inputs clamp and take more than 100 mA without
   latch-up (adi-ltc2293-2292-2291-datasheet p.5, note 3), which is why the 2-RX board is the
   reference; the LP5907 turn-on time 80 typ / 150 max us (ti-lp5907-datasheet p.6); the LD39200
   EN thresholds (1.2 V high, 0.5 V low, st-ld39200-datasheet p.7), PG threshold and start-up
   plots (p.13, Figures 21-24); the FPGA pull-ups during configuration (amd-ug470-7series-config
   p.22, amd-ds181-artix7-datasheet p.3, 90-330 uA); the U6 PL133-37 output levels
   (microchip-pl133-37-datasheet). The FPGA: the recommended power-on order VCCINT, VCCBRAM,
   VCCAUX, VCCO, power-off in reverse, and VCCO - VCCAUX not above 2.625 V for longer than
   TVCCO2VCCAUX per cycle (amd-ds181-artix7-datasheet p.8 and the TVCCO2VCCAUX table); on this
   board VCCINT and VCCBRAM are 1V0 (U17), VCCAUX and VCCADC 1V8 (U23), VCCO 3V3D (U25). The
   bucks U17 (1V0), U18 (3V8) and U19 (5V6) are LMR51420s with EN on 12 V and no power-good
   output: their EN thresholds and soft-start times (ti-lmr51420-datasheet). Bypass capacitors from the `kicad-1rx/` netlist with the
   rx1-02 changes (C993-C995 10u 0805, R992 10k in PDWN, R91/R92 5.1k/12k).
2. `analysis/15_rx1_powerup/sequence.py` and its output `sequence.csv`: the order map. One row
   per ordering the ADC and the FPGA need (3V0 before 3V3A, 1V8 and 3V3D; VCCINT before VCCBRAM,
   VCCAUX and VCCO; VCCAUX before VCCO; the VCCO - VCCAUX limit; each power-off order), read from
   the `kicad-1rx/` netlist with the rx1-02 changes: what enables each regulator, and whether the
   ordering is `design` (an EN or PG chain forces it) or `timing` (it holds only because one
   regulator is faster), with the margin the datasheet times give. For every `timing` row,
   propose a design fix with its cost (parts, nets, space, side effects): an EN from the earlier
   rail where its voltage clears the EN threshold, an EN divider, a PG chain, or a supervisor IC;
   check each against the EN thresholds of step 1. Today 1V0 (U17) before 1V8 (U23) is `timing`:
   both trace back to bucks enabled from 12 V. Every proposed fix that adds or moves a connection
   is run through `analysis/11_board_1rx/route_space.py` (add it to `CHANGES`): the route length,
   vias and layers on today's board, or that no route exists; a fix that adds a part also gets a
   placement search like `cap_space.py`. (Amended 2026-11-25 at the user's request: check that
   the board has space for each trace change.)
3. `[heavy]` `analysis/15_rx1_powerup/powerup_sim.py`: LTspice transient through `./run --sim`.
   3V8 ramps as the U18 buck starts (its soft start, ti-lmr51420-datasheet), and 1V0 (U17) and
   5V6 (U19) from their soft starts the same way; U39 from the TI LP5907-3.0 model on file
   (ti-lp5907-3p0-pspice); U24, U23 and U25 as LD39200 behavioural
   models (EN threshold, start-up ramp fitted to the p.13 plots, PG at its threshold; ST publishes
   no model, parts/models_needed.csv) with U24 EN and U23 EN on 3V0 and U25 EN on U23 PG; U45 the
   LT6231 subcircuit with its bias from R990/R991; R97/R99 49.9 ohm and C163-C165; U6 as a 3.3 V
   CMOS source on 3V3D with a stated output resistance (no vendor model), through R15 33 ohm, with
   Y2's start-up stated as an assumption; PDWN from a 3V3D pull-up of 90-330 uA through R992;
   each AD9237 pin as its capacitance with clamp diodes to AVDD and AGND, and AVDD's own load.
   Cases: power-up with each regulator at its fastest and slowest datasheet time; power-down by
   12 V removal; each with the mixer IF at zero and at full scale; and CLK in steady state with
   3V3D and 3V0 at their tolerance limits. Heavy because: five regulators with no common model
   set, and the clamp current is sensitive to the diode model and the U6 output resistance, which
   are assumptions to be stated and swept. Record each rail's start and end time, so the order
   map of step 2 is checked against the simulation.
4. Pass when, in every case, each pin stays within AGND - 0.3 V and AVDD + 0.3 V, and 3V3A, 1V8
   and 3V3D start only after 3V0 passes the EN threshold. Otherwise report the peak clamp current
   and its duration, say that the AD9237 sheet gives no current it tolerates, and propose fixes
   with their cost for the user to choose: for CLK a larger R15 or a divider at CLK (with the
   clock edge and duty cycle at 40 MHz rerun); for VIN a larger series resistance (with the
   settling and IF response of rx1-02 rerun) or a Schottky clamp to 3V0; for the order a
   supervisor on 3V0 (`rx1-seq` B). The FPGA order passes when 1V0, 1V8 and 3V3D come up and go
   down in the DS181 order and VCCO - VCCAUX stays within its limit and time.
5. `tests/test_rx1_powerup.py`: the pass check on fixture waveforms (a pin above AVDD + 0.3 V
   fails, a rail starting before 3V0 fails, an FPGA rail out of order fails, VCCO - VCCAUX above
   2.625 V for too long fails), the order map on a fixture netlist (an EN on 12 V is `timing`, an
   EN on a PG is `design`), and the LD39200 ramp fit against the plot points read
   from the datasheet figure.
6. Report `writeup.md` and `deck.yaml` (no more than 12 slides): the order map (design or timing,
   with the fix for each timing row), the rail waveforms in order, the
   VIN, CLK and PDWN voltages against AVDD + 0.3 V per case, the clamp currents, the CLK steady
   state, the result and any fix; the waveforms lay-03 should match at bring-up. Add the result
   to the `U24, U23 enables from 3V0` and U8 rows of `docs/board-changes.md`. A chosen fix becomes
   a row of `parts/rx1_changes.csv` for rx1-03.

## Acceptance
- Every case of step 2 is shown, with the peak voltage at VIN+, VIN-, CLK and PDWN against
  AVDD + 0.3 V and the order in which 3V0, 3V3A, 1V8 and 3V3D come up.
- The diode model, the LD39200 behavioural model, Y2's start-up and the U6 output resistance are
  stated as assumptions, with their sweep.
- The CLK steady state at the 3V3D and 3V0 tolerance limits is shown.
- The order map lists every ADC and FPGA ordering, on and off, as design or timing; every timing
  row has a design fix with its cost, or says why none exists.
- Every proposed fix states whether today's board has room for it (route_space.py and, for a new
  part, a placement search).
- The FPGA rails are shown against the DS181 order and the VCCO - VCCAUX limit.
- A failing case names its fixes and their cost; no fix is applied here.
