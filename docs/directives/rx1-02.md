## Goal
The user can approve every change that turns the 2-RX board into the 1-RX board in one review,
with no open question that would hold up Friday's order:
- U8 becomes the AD9237BCPZ-40 (LCSC C514275);
- the second receive chain is not fitted;
- every placed line is in JLCPCB stock for `board_quantity` boards.

Each change has its evidence, the check that covers it, and the layout work it causes. It holds
when the report builds, every change cites a downloaded datasheet page, and every choice is in
`parts/choices.json`.

## Steps
1. Sources:
   - record the AD9237 datasheet the user downloaded (Rev. C, `datasheets/files/adi/ad9237.pdf`,
     already copied) in `datasheets/manifest.yaml`, with its sha256;
   - add the ADS5231 (TI) and FT2232H (FTDI) datasheets through `tools/fetch_sources.py`;
   - anything that cannot be fetched goes to `docs/user-fetch.md`.
2. ADC pin map and straps, from the AD9237 datasheet (Table 6 on p.8, Tables 7-10), each line
   with its page:
   - Map each U8 net in `kicad/` to an AD9237 pin, or mark it "removed". The nets are 3V0, 3V3D,
     ADC_CLK, D0-D11, OFA, ADC_OE1, ADC_SHDN1, AINA+/-, REFHA/REFLA, SENSEA, MODE and VCMA. All
     channel-B nets are removed.
   - Set SENSE, MODE2 and VREF so the input span matches the span U45 drives today (read from the
     LTC2292 family sheet and `opamp_amp.kicad_sch`).
   - Set MODE and GC so the output format is the one the firmware expects, and set the polarity of
     OE and PDWN against how the FPGA drives ADC_OE1 and ADC_SHDN1. Read both from `adc.vhd` and
     the constraints in `davidmhunt/fmcw3-ckflight-fw` at a commit recorded in the report. That
     repo is read only.
   - Decoupling per the datasheet.
   - A 1.5 V common-mode bias for U45 channel A, which replaces the LTC2292's VCMA output: a
     divider from 3V0 with decoupling, sized against U45's input current.
3. [heavy] Analysis of the new ADC input, covering board-changes rows that have no simulation
   yet. Heavy because: a new numerical model of the ADC driver and its load.
   - LTspice in `./run --sim`: U45 channel A (the 1-RX op-amp from step 5, its vendor model)
     drives the AD9237 input through the existing network. Model the input load from the
     datasheet's equivalent input circuit, and give the bias from the step 2 divider.
   - Report:
     - peak swing against the span, with margin;
     - common-mode at the pins;
     - settling within half a 40 MHz period;
     - the IF response over the band against today's design.
   - 3V0 rail load: the AD9237 against the LTC2292 current, against the regulator's rating.
   - SNR and dynamic range: AD9237 at 40 MSPS against the LTC2292, in dB, and what it means for
     the IF dynamic range.
4. RX2 not fitted:
   - From the `kicad/` netlist, list every part used only by receive chain 2, from its antenna
     port to ADC channel B. Those become DNP in 1-RX.
   - Show that no shared part (a dual mixer, a dual op-amp, the LO split) is removed.
   - List the FPGA pins freed.
5. JLC stock only:
   - Using a fresh JLC database (`./run parts/check_jlc.py --refresh`, date in the report),
     check every placed 1-RX line for JLC stock of at least 3 times its quantity. That covers
     both routing and parts that are already fitted.
   - Each line that fails gets JLC-stocked options. The known ones from par-09 are U30, U26-U28,
     U45, U21, P1/P2/U15, L5-L7 and U29 (stock equals need).
   - Classify each option drop-in, footprint change or design change. Cite its datasheet page
     and its check, for example:
     - the L5-L7 saturation current against the LMR51420 current limit;
     - the U21 feedback divider for 10 V;
     - the U45 replacement's model in the step 3 simulation.
   - Reuse the user's par-03 and par-09 choices where they already fit.
   - Report the count of footprint changes the layout must take.
6. Data path for real-time SAR (the user's question, 2026-11-22). Compare:
   - the 1-RX ADC data rate, both raw (40 MSPS x 12 bit) and after the firmware's decimation;
   - the FT2232H synchronous FIFO rate (datasheet page);
   - the FPGA's buffer resources.

   Say whether raw or decimated samples can stream off the board with no hardware change, and
   name the hardware change that would be needed if not. Making that change is out of scope.
7. Record:
   - `analysis/11_board_1rx/` writeup, deck and figures; build the report;
   - 1-RX choices in `parts/choices.json` (keys prefixed `rx1-`);
   - `parts/rx1_changes.csv`: one row per ref or net change, which rx1-03 applies and tests
     against;
   - rows in `docs/board-changes.md` with a new "Board" column (`1-RX`, `2-RX` or `both`), each
     naming its covering check.

- The figures regenerate from `analysis/11_board_1rx/figures.py`.

  option.

- Audience: the user. It fits in 15 slides or fewer.
