## Goal
Every BOM line has a proposed exact part number with its evidence, and the open choices are laid
out for the user to decide. It holds when `parts/decisions.csv` covers every placed line and the
`01_parts` report and deck build.

## Steps
1. For each line par-01's live run left without an exact match (upstream's old run had 20
   `No-result` lines), find the orderable suffix from the datasheet ordering table whose package
   matches the footprint; record part, chosen code, datasheet manifest id and page, and reason in
   `parts/decisions.csv`. The datasheet must be in `datasheets/manifest.yaml` (par-02) and read
   from the downloaded file, never from memory. A part whose datasheet is still pending in
   `docs/user-fetch.md`, or that no source matched, is listed in the report as "for the user to
   obtain" with what is missing, never guessed. Use LCSC numbers found on upstream's JLCPCB branches
   (survey fnd-01) as a starting point, each re-checked.
2. Prepare, but do not decide, the user's choices, each with options, stock, price and the
   consequence: XC7A35T or XC7A100T (U30): the schematic is the 100T, `production/` is the as-ordered 35T, the
   firmware has bitstreams for both and the FTG256 footprint is shared, so also check that every
   U30 pin the board uses exists on the 35T (bank and pin validity) (survey: `docs/surveys/upstream-survey.md`); LTC2292 C or I grade (U8); USB-C receptacle for the
   `GCT_USB4105` footprint; replacement for `SRR6040A-330M` (L5-L7) with saturation current
   checked against the LMR51420 peak current; any part JLCPCB cannot assemble; whether `kicad/libs/fmcw3.lib` (retired part ideas,
   kept until this goal is done) is still needed.
3. Propose an MPN for each generic passive group (tolerance, voltage, dielectric stated; C0G for
   RF and filter capacitors), preferring JLCPCB Basic parts.
4. Run `./run parts/bom.py` live (board count from the target set's `board_quantity`, 3 in
   `default.yaml` as of 2026-11-21) and commit the outputs under `parts/out/`.
   `parts/check_decisions.py` exits 0 only when every placed line has a decision row or an
   entry in the user-choices list.
5. `analysis/01_parts/` (`writeup.md`, `deck.yaml`, `figures.py`): report and deck. When the
   project is open in KiCad, use `tools/kicad_ipc.py show --refs ...` to point at the parts of
   each choice while discussing it. Slides: status summary chart, the lines JLCPCB can
   assemble versus consign versus hand-solder, each user choice on its own slide, obsolete or
   risky parts, cost for N boards from both sources.

## Acceptance
- Every placed BOM line appears once in the report tables with MPN, DigiKey status and JLCPCB status.
- Each suffix decision cites the datasheet and page.
- Each user choice has a slide with at least two options and a recommendation labelled as such.
- Figures and tables are generated by `analysis/01_parts/figures.py` from `parts/out/`.
- The deck has no more than 20 slides.

## Review
- Open `reports/01_parts.pptx`. Decide each choice slide (FPGA, ADC grade, USB-C, inductor,
  non-assemblable parts) and give the decisions in the note when you accept this directive.

## Out of scope
- Schematic edits (par-04).
- Ordering.

## Allowed commands
- `./run *`

- Check: against the strict datasheet rule (Isat above 3.5 A typ, 5.1 A max) the SRR6040A-330M at about 1.0 A fails by a factor of 3.5 to 5. In normal operation the peak is the load plus half the ripple, so 1.0 A would allow roughly 0.9 to 1.0 A of load per rail before saturating; any overload or short or the start-up on a rail loaded above that drives the peak to the 3.5 A limit and the inductor saturates (hiccup mode limits the average, not the peak).
- 33 uH is also far above what the datasheet asks: with KIND 20 to 60 % of 2 A (page 18) the ripple would be 0.4 to 1.2 A, which for 12 V in gives L(min) for KIND 0.3 of about 1.4 uH (1V0), 3.9 uH (3V8), 4.5 uH (5V6) at 1.1 MHz and 3.1 / 8.7 / 9.9 uH at 500 kHz (same equation 11). A smaller inductance is a value change the user decides; it also gets a higher Isat in the same body.
- Options: (A) keep SRR6040A-330M: consign 9 (or more) from DigiKey NFND stock 777, USD 0.95 each; last-time-buy risk, Isat 1.0 A below the 3.5 A limit. (B) 33 uH with Isat above 3.5 A: from the JLC 33 uH power-inductor rows the ones whose two listed currents both reach 3.5 A or more are 10 to 12 mm bodies: APS1040M330A style parts (COILANK APS1040M330A C47327250 4.44 A and 5.05 A, 11.5x10 mm, stock 19638, USD 0.241; cjiang FXL1040-330-M C475918 4.4 A and 5 A, USD 0.272; ZEMS1040-330M C41413144 4.4 and 5 A, USD 0.205; SMMS1040-330M C149598 3.5 and 5 A, USD 0.201; PSPMAA1040H-330M C436575 4 and 6 A, 11x10 mm, USD 0.237). (C) 33 uH, 7x6.6 mm molded, same pad region probably but Isat 2 to 2.8 A, below 3.5 A: SMS0650-330MT C5127476 (2.8 and 3.3 A, stock 21335, USD 0.094), SMMS0650-330M C149587 (2.3 and 3.3 A, stock 23573, USD 0.13), ZEMS0650-330M C41415622 (2.8 and 3.3 A).
